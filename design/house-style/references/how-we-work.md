# How we work

How Joe likes design work done on these projects.

## Showing work

- Lead with the result. Short answers, bullet points, tables and pictures over long paragraphs.
- Show options side by side on one picture sheet, labelled A, B, C, each with a one-line note, then recommend one.
- Show changes as screenshots: phone and desktop, before and after when it helps.
- Say plainly what was not checked, and flag any wording you wrote that the owner has not confirmed.

## Design choices

- Polished and professional, not cheesy. Say more with less.
- Rainbow colour is an accent: a word, a dot, a line, a ring. Never a big fill.
- Motion is smooth and purposeful. No bounce. Things that repeat fade back gently before they start again.
- Real photos of real work. No AI imagery standing in for the business's own work.
- Never alter a logo without being asked. Concepts stay concepts until chosen.
- Keep personal things private: no personal photos or details on public pages unless the owner asks.

## Iterating

- Small, precise changes. Change one thing, show it, then the next.
- If a change makes something worse, put it back the way it was straight away.
- Measure alignment and spacing; two pixels out is visible.
