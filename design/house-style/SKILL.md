# Simple & Two Points House Style

Professional, with a touch of flair. Mostly ink, grey and white. Colour is rare, and when it appears it means something.

## Before you design

1. Read `references/brand-book.md`. It is the main rulebook.
2. Brand work for Two Points: also read `references/two-points.md`. Email or signature: `references/email.md`. A website: `references/websites.md`.
3. Use the real values: `references/tokens.css` (CSS variables, light and dark) or `references/tokens.json`. Never guess a colour.
4. Build from `references/components.md` and `references/components.css`.
5. Use the original logo files in `assets/logos`. Never redraw or alter a logo unless the owner asks.
6. Follow `references/how-we-work.md` when showing work.

## The rules that matter most

- **Colour is rare.** Ink on white, grey details. The rainbow is a thin line, a ring, one word, a dot. Never a big fill or a filled button.
- **Each brand has its own rainbow.** Simple: lime to violet. Two Points: Alpenglow, teal to pink. Everything else is shared.
- **The letterhead:** a dark band on top, a plain page between, a dark band at the bottom.
- **Headings:** 800 weight, tight tracking. On websites, two tones: a statement in ink, a quiet part in grey ending in one rainbow word and a gradient full stop.
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans only (400 to 800).
- **Buttons:** Simple uses the glow button (rainbow edge, faint glow). Two Points uses the ring button (solid pill, 1.5px Alpenglow ring).
- **Motion:** things arrive once, smoothly, no bounce. Nothing blocks reading. Respect reduced motion.
- **Voice:** short, plain, specific. Claim only what is true. No digs at competitors. Flag wording the owner has not confirmed.
- **Email:** tables, inline styles, hosted 2x pictures, fixed widths. Gmail strips flexible widths from signatures.
- **Pictures:** real work photos. No AI imagery standing in for real work.
