On your phone

The Gmail app on iPhone only allows a plain-text mobile signature and strips out pictures. Instead, let it use the signature above:

  1. Set the signature above in Gmail on a computer first.
  2. In the Gmail app: Settings → your account → Signature settings.
  3. Turn Mobile signature off.
  4. Send yourself a test email from your phone. The full signature is added when the email is sent. It does not show while you are typing.
  • Gmail on a computer: paste as above. Images and links come with it.
  • Outlook or Apple Mail: paste into the signature settings the same way.
  • For another team member, ask for a copy with their name, title and phone.
