  • Gmail on a computer: paste as above. Images and links come with it.
  • Gmail app on a phone: it uses its own plain-text signature. Set the signature on a computer and emails sent from the phone still use it when sent through Gmail on the web.
  • Outlook or Apple Mail: paste into the signature settings the same way.
  • For another team member, ask for a copy with their name, title and phone.
