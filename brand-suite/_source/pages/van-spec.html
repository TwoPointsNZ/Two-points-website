Type

All lettering is Plus Jakarta Sans (free from Google Fonts). Phone number: ExtraBold 800, letters tightened 3%. Website: Bold 700. Small capitals: Bold 700, spaced out 18%. The logo is supplied as vector artwork. Do not redraw it or set it in another typeface.

Checked before it goes on

This layout assumes: white van · short wheelbase · low roof · sliding door on the kerb side · twin rear doors. If the van has a tailgate, windows or a second sliding door, the same five parts move to the nearest flat panel. Optional extras: the mountain mark in white on the bonnet centre (300 mm wide), and reflective vinyl for the two lines.
