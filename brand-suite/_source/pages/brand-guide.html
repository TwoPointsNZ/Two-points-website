Brand guide.
The short version.

The one rule

Colour is rare, and it means something

Mostly ink, grey and white. Alpenglow shows up only as a thin line, a ring, one word or a full stop. If a design looks colourful, take colour out.

The logo

Never redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects. Keep clear space of one "O" all round. Smallest size: 20 mm wide in print, 24 px tall on screen. Never on a photo without a dark band behind it.

The two ropes

New in this suite: the two ropes. Two ring anchors, two ropes, a backup device and a descender, both holding one technician. The technician is the brand full stop. It is the number one rule of rope access in one picture. Use it once per piece: the van, business card C, the LinkedIn banner. Where a page can't print to the edge (letterhead C, the envelope) the ropes stop with a stopper knot instead. Never more than one pair, never horizontal, never as a pattern.

Brand guide · colour and type
Alpenglow

Always all four, in this order, as a fade. Stops at 0, 38, 70 and 100%. The full stop uses the last two. For print: these are screen colours. Ask the printer for a proof, because bright teal and pink dull down in standard four-colour print.

Neutrals

Red (#b42318 on #fde7e7) only ever means something is waiting or owed. Safety yellow appears only on site warning signs.

Words

Plain, short, specific. New Zealand English. No exclamation marks. Claim only what is true today. Letters and emails end "Regards,". The tagline is "Because ladders only go so far."

Brand guide · using it
The letterhead layout

Dark band, white page, dark band

Every piece starts here. Logo on the band. A thin Alpenglow line where the band meets the page. Inside the page: lines and space, not boxes.

Do and don't
Use the original logo files. White on the band, ink on white.
Colour the logo, tilt it, outline it or put it on a busy photo.
Keep Alpenglow thin: a line, a ring, one word, a full stop.
Fill a background, a button or a panel with the rainbow.
Use real job photos. Yours, from real sites.
Use stock or computer-made photos of rope work. Clients can tell.
Say what is true today: IRATA Level 3, SiteWise Gold 2026/27.
Claim a certificate or service not held, or knock another trade.
Put Two Points on everything a client sees.
Put the Simple logo on a client document. Simple is the software.

Printing

Files are supplied in screen colour (RGB) with 3 mm bleed and crop marks. Ask for a digital print with a proof first. Good stock: 400 gsm uncoated or soft-touch matt for cards, 150 gsm silk for flyers, 120 gsm uncoated for letterheads.
