Putting one into Gmail

  1. Press Copy under the footer you want.
  2. Gmail on a computer: Settings (gear) → See all settings → Signature → Create new → paste → Save changes.
  3. On your iPhone: Gmail app → Settings → your account → Signature settings → turn Mobile signature off, so the full footer is used.
  4. For a crew member: change the boxes at the top first, then copy.
