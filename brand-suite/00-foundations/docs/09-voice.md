# 9 · Words

The same voice everywhere: website, documents, emails, apps.

## How it sounds

- **Plain, short, specific.**
  - Good: "Review St Andrew's College completion form."
  - Not: "Action required: pending submission review."
- **Say what matters first.** The headline is the news ("3 things need you"), not a greeting.
- **Short sentences.** One idea each. Bullets over paragraphs.
- **New Zealand English:** colour, organise, metres, "Christchurch", dates as **24 Nov 2026** or **Tue 24 Nov**, NZD with GST stated.
- **Friendly, not chummy.** Confident, not salesy. No exclamation marks in client documents.
- **No jargon to clients:**
  - "Safety plan (SSSP)" the first time, then "SSSP".
  - "Rope access", not "IRATA L3 suspended operations".

## Words we use

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| Site completion report | Job sheet, closeout doc |
| Quote | Proposal, estimate (unless it is one) |
| Crew / technicians | Resources, staff |
| Waiting · Overdue · Ready to send · Clear · Done | Pending · Outstanding · Actioned |
| "Open" › | "Click here" |

## Client sign-off

- Emails end **"Regards,"**.
- The signature card gives name, Director, phone, email and website.

## Legal

- Client documents show **Two Points Ltd** (the company name), GST at 15%, "excl. GST" on each line and the total incl. GST.
- The "Simple" name never appears on a client document.
