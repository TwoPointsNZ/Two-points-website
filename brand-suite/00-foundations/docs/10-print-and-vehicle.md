# 10 · Print, vehicle, social and site

Added 25 November 2026 with the brand suite. Everything here is **Two Points** (Alpenglow, mountain logo). Simple never appears.

## The two ropes

A graphic device made for print and the van. Drawn by `_source/ropes.js` (one source for every piece), the way Two Points rigs them (Joe, 26 Nov 2026).

- **Top:** a grey bolt hanger and carabiner at each anchor. The bunny ears are real loops through the carabiners, into a solid figure 8 knot drawn like the stopper (filled, with its two crossing turns marked). (A figure-8-loop version, `eights`, and a shared-anchor version, `shared`, are also in the file.)
- **Bottom:** a stopper knot with a tail left below it. The pink rope hangs a little lower than the blue one.
- **Colour:** rope 1 fades teal → blue, rope 2 indigo → pink. Anchors are grey: they are not rope.
- **Rules:**
  - always the whole rig, anchor to tail, with clear space round it. It never runs off an edge and is never cropped;
  - once per piece, always vertical;
  - ropes are thin: about 0.4 mm on a card, 16 mm on the van;
  - never horizontal, never a pattern, never a second pair;
  - a piece with the ropes has no other Alpenglow fill.
- **Where:** van, business card C, letterhead C, envelope, ID card, LinkedIn banner, social post 2.
- **Tried and dropped:** plain stripes running off the edge; ring anchors with devices and a "technician" dot. Joe did not like either.

- PDFs come out of Chromium in **RGB**. Printers convert. Ask for a proof: bright teal and pink dull down.
- Gradient words in these PDFs are real gradients (fine in Chromium PDFs). The html2canvas rule (colour letter by letter) still applies to documents made inside Simple.
- Minimum logo width in print: 20 mm.

## Vehicle

- Three options: A Letterhead, B Two lines (recommended), C Headline.
- Black cut vinyl for the logo and phone. Dark grey cut vinyl for small words. The two ropes are digitally printed and laminated.
- On a dark van: white logo, light grey small words, the same ropes.
- Keep every part off handles, rails, rub strips and panel gaps.
- The phone number is always the biggest lettering after the logo.

## Site and workwear

- Clothing: black or charcoal with a white logo. Ink logo on hi-vis. No coloured logos on clothing.
- Left chest 90 mm. Back 280 mm.
- One optional accent per garment: a 3 mm Alpenglow line at the hem or cuff.
- Site signs use safety yellow and black. That is the only place a non-brand colour appears.
