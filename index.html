Our services

Multiple trades. One set of ropes.

01

Façade repairs and installation

We carry out structural and cosmetic repairs to a wide range of façade types, including cladding, panels and fixings. We also install new façade systems using rope access, a safe, efficient and cost-effective approach for complex high-access work.

02

Anchor installation and certification

We provide professional anchor installation and certification for rope access and height safety systems, ensuring all anchor points are installed, tested and certified to meet relevant safety standards.

03

Glazing

Our team handles the installation and replacement of glazing at height, from single panes to large-format curtain wall systems, including resealing and leak-proofing as required.

04

Sealant work

We specialise in the application and replacement of exterior sealants, including joints, expansion gaps and perimeter seals, using weather-resistant and UV-stable products for long-lasting protection against leaks and water ingress.

05

Painting

We provide professional painting services using rope access techniques, allowing safe and efficient access to difficult or high-level areas. Ideal for exterior maintenance, touch-ups and full surface coatings.

06

Signage

We install signs, banners and lighting systems on buildings, towers and other high-access structures, with minimal disruption to building operations and full compliance with safety standards.
