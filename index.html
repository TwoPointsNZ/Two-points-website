Why rope access

Rope access reaches the places scaffolding can't, without the cost, the wait or the disruption. We rig, descend, do the work and leave your building running the whole time.

Two independent lines on every technician, under IRATA Level 3 supervision.

Our services 6

Six trades.
One set of ropes.

We carry out structural and cosmetic repairs to a wide range of façade types, including cladding, panels and fixings. We also install new façade systems using rope access: a safer, faster and more cost-effective alternative to scaffolding for complex high-access work.

Anchor points for rope access and height safety systems, installed, tested and certified to meet the relevant safety standards. It keeps your building compliant, reliable and safe to access for maintenance and operational work at height.

Installation and replacement of glazing at height, from single panes to large-format curtain wall systems. With expert rigging and precise positioning, high-level glazing is completed safely and efficiently, including resealing and leak-proofing as required.

Application and replacement of exterior sealants: joints, expansion gaps and perimeter seals. Weather-resistant, UV-stable products give long-lasting protection against leaks and water ingress, especially on exposed façades and glazing systems.

Professional painting using rope access techniques, reaching difficult or high-level areas without scaffolding. Ideal for exterior maintenance, touch-ups and full surface coatings on buildings where conventional access is limited.

Signs, banners and lighting systems on buildings, towers and other high-access structures. Permanent signage or temporary promotional material, rigged and placed with minimal disruption to building operations.
