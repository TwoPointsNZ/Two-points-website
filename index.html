Our services 6

Six trades. One set of ropes.

ServiceTP-01

Façade repairs and installation

We carry out structural and cosmetic repairs to a wide range of façade types, including cladding, panels and fixings. We also install new façade systems using rope access: a safer, faster and more cost-effective alternative to scaffolding for complex high-access work.

Quote this job
ServiceTP-02

Anchor installation and certification

We provide professional anchor installation and certification for rope access and height safety systems, ensuring all anchor points are installed, tested and certified to meet relevant safety standards. It supports ongoing compliance, reliability and safe access for maintenance and operational work at height.

Quote this job
ServiceTP-03

Glazing

Our team handles the installation and replacement of glazing at height, from single panes to large-format curtain wall systems. With expert rigging and precise positioning, we complete high-level glazing work safely and efficiently, including resealing and leak-proofing as required.

Quote this job
ServiceTP-04

Sealant work

We specialise in the application and replacement of exterior sealants, including joints, expansion gaps and perimeter seals. Using weather-resistant and UV-stable products, we deliver long-lasting protection against leaks and water ingress, especially important for exposed façades and glazing systems.

Quote this job
ServiceTP-05

Painting

We provide professional painting services using rope access techniques, allowing safe and efficient access to difficult or high-level areas without the need for scaffolding. Ideal for exterior maintenance, touch-ups and full surface coatings on buildings where conventional access is limited.

Quote this job
ServiceTP-06

Signage

We install signs, banners and lighting systems on buildings, towers and other high-access structures. Whether it's permanent signage or temporary promotional material, we manage the rigging and placement with minimal disruption to building operations and full compliance with safety standards.
